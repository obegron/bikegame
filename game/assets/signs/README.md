# Generated shop signs

These eight storefront illustrations were made for Bikegame with OpenAI's
built-in image generation, then resized to 1024×512 and given deterministic
shop lettering with ImageMagick.

Common prompt direction:

> Horizontal 2:1 vintage hand-painted European storefront sign for a cheerful
> stylized low-poly cycling courier game. Ornamental heritage border, bold
> central food emblem, coordinated limited palette, weathered painted surface,
> clear lower banner, readable at a distance. No text, letters, logo, or
> watermark.

The individual subjects were:

- Market Hall — produce basket with bread, cheese, and vegetables; green,
  cream, and gold.
- Boulangerie du Pont — baguette, wheat, and a stone bridge; navy, cream, and
  gold.
- Old Mill Bakery — watermill wheel, loaves, and flour sack; walnut, cream,
  and slate.
- South Gate Burger Bar — stacked artisan burger and medieval gate; burgundy,
  cream, and gold.
- Quayside Pizzeria — margherita pizza, basil, tomatoes, and water; blue and
  ivory.
- North Bank Sushi — nigiri, maki, chopsticks, and waves; indigo and ivory.
- Hilltop Thai Kitchen — curry, rice, lime, basil, chilli, and hills; emerald
  and gold.
- Orchard Farm Shop — apples, pears, berries, and preserves; green and cream.

The final uppercase store names were added after generation so spelling remains
exact and legible in the game.
