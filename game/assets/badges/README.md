# Generated achievement badges

These six 512×512 badge illustrations were made for Bikegame with OpenAI's
built-in image generation. Their connected neutral backgrounds were removed
after generation so they sit cleanly on the dark game UI.

Common prompt direction:

> Square 1:1 polished game UI achievement badge / enamel medallion for a
> cheerful stylized low-poly European cycling courier game. Centered emblem,
> strong clean silhouette, chunky readable shapes, subtle bevel and dimensional
> shading, crisp edges, readable at 64 pixels, fills most of the canvas. Plain
> neutral background. No text, letters, numbers, logo, or watermark.

The individual emblem prompts were:

- First delivery — wrapped parcel, bicycle wheel, and laurel; bronze, red,
  cream, and navy.
- First kilometre — European milestone, wheel, and curving road; silver,
  cobalt, cream, and navy.
- City explorer — old-European skyline, arched bridge, map, and compass; teal
  and antique gold.
- Ten active minutes — stopwatch, heart-shaped cycling route, and pedals; green
  and gold.
- Around the clock — half sun, half moon clock with stars and bicycle; violet,
  blue, gold, cream, and coral.
- Five-star streak — parcel, winged wheel, laurel, and exactly five stars;
  gold, cream, and navy.
